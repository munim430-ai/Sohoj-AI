THE NUMBERS

STEPPS Model — Correlation with Purchase Intention (r)
Top Performer
Emotion (Storytelling) leads at r = 0.78, mean 4.62/5. Use real-life youth routines and genuine emotional arcs — not scripted ads.
Strong Signal
Social Currency at r = 0.72, mean 4.45/5. Content must help users look creative, trend-aware, and part of a cultural movement.
Moderate Signal
Practical Value at r = 0.65. Low-cost cold recipes, dorm-room hacks, and exam-night rituals drive both trial and repeat sharing.
Weakest Signal
Functional Ads at r = 0.22. Shift investment from product-fact content to creator-led emotional storytelling immediately.
Sensory & Sustainability Metrics — Mean Score out of 5
Sustainability Imperative
Transparent EFPs scored 4.85/5 with 89% strongly agreeing — the single highest metric in the entire study. Make it visible and specific.
Visual Priority
81% strongly agree that visual aesthetics drive engagement. Every content asset must be designed for maximum scroll-stopping impact.
Boycott Risk
74% would boycott brands without visible eco-claims. Vague "green" language is worse than silence — communicate verifiable proof only.
Sonic Branding
ASMR ice sounds, pouring shots, and preparation sounds scored 4.35/5. Audio is an underused engagement lever for coffee content.

Campaign Strategy

START COLD,
SHARE BOLD

Position Nescafé as a creative, cold-ready, sensory-rich coffee companion that helps young people express identity, build routines, and participate in sustainable coffee culture.


Make it cold. Make it visual. Make it useful. Make it shareable. Make the sustainability proof visible.

01
🎬
Creator-Led Content
Campus micro-creators producing 10–20s recipe transformation videos. Native platform style beats every polished corporate ad for Gen Z trust.
02
🧊
Cold Coffee Recipes
Iced latte, dorm-room mocha, exam-night frappe — affordable rituals that turn daily study moments into shareable coffee experiences.
03
🏫
Campus Activation
Challenge packs, ambassador bundles, Bangla-English captions. Affordable trial kits that make Nescafé the go-to campus coffee companion.
04
🌿
Sustainability Proof
Translate Nescafé Plan 2030 into simple, localized proof points. QR-linked eco-explainers on packaging make sustainability undeniable.
05
🔊
Sensory Engagement
ASMR ice sounds, close-up pour shots, sound marks. Coffee preparation as premium content — make every cup worth watching and sharing.
Implementation Roadmap

5-STEP
ACTIVATION PLAN

A focused 12-week brand revitalization sprint — from audience diagnosis to measurable campaign results.

01
Weeks 1 – 2
DIAGNOSE
Gen Z content audit and pulse survey. Build updated audience insight sheet for campaign targeting.
02
Weeks 3 – 5
DESIGN
Recruit campus micro-creators, test recipe videos. Target 15–30 short-form content assets.
03
Weeks 6 – 8
ACTIVATE
Launch cold coffee recipes and bundle offers. Deploy trial coupons and conversion tracking.
04
Weeks 6 – 10
PROVE
Publish sustainability proof assets on packaging and digital channels. QR-linked eco-explainer pages.
05
Weeks 11 – 12
MEASURE
Compare KPIs, identify best-performing formats, deliver campaign learning report for next quarter.
